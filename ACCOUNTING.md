# Loomi accounting

Accounting includes categorized income, bills and credits; partial payments; refundable security deposits; accrual Profit & Loss; cash flow; monthly comparisons; guest balances; property, currency and date filters; and CSV exports.

Accommodation revenue uses completed nights of checked-in or checked-out reservations. Payments for future stays are advances. Opening balances do not count as new cash receipts or extra income. Refundable security deposits are money held, excluded from profit.

Enter a bill or other income on its incurred or earned date, then record actual payments separately. Do not enter accommodation income again: reservations already supply it. A reservation reference on a manual record does not change the guest's reservation balance. Use income or expense credits to reduce recognized amounts; settle the credit when money is refunded.

Currencies remain separate. Current balances are shown as of today, independently of the selected report period. Older manual cash entries use their original dates until categorized.

These are management reports based on saved reservation dates/rates and entered accounting records. Historical results can change if reservations change. This is not a complete QuickBooks replacement: bank reconciliation, immutable closing periods, balance sheets and automated tax/depreciation calculations are not included.
