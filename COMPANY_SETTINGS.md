# Company and appearance settings

Settings → Property & rooms (or House details) includes Appearance & company details. Settings apply to the selected property and are saved in its existing property record. Staff need Edit hotel name and currency permission (Property) to change them.

Choose an app accent color with the color picker or a six-digit hex value. The picker previews across the app immediately. Save color keeps the color independently of other company fields; Save appearance & company details keeps all changes. Leaving Settings without saving restores the saved color. Buttons, sidebar, focus outlines, links and reserved calendar cells use the accent. Text colors are chosen for contrast. Reset selects Loomi's default green.

Upload a PNG, JPG or WebP logo up to 5 MB. The browser resizes it to at most 512 pixels, and the app saves an embedded image up to 256 KB. Preview or remove the logo before saving. Uploaded logos appear on printed and downloaded POS receipts and reservation invoices.

Company fields include trading/legal name, address, phone, email, website, registration and tax numbers, receipt footer, invoice notes/terms and bank/payment details. Leaving company name blank uses the property name. Receipts use company contact/registration details and the receipt footer. Invoice notes and payment details appear on reservation invoices.

Open a saved reservation and select View invoice. The invoice includes accommodation, posted POS charges assigned to that reservation, applied payments and the remaining balance. Refunded POS orders are excluded. Unallocated accommodation overpayments are shown separately. Print to paper or choose Save as PDF in the browser print dialog; Download invoice saves a standalone HTML file.

Documents are generated from current saved details. They are not immutable issued-invoice records or a fiscal invoicing system. Save reservation changes before generating an invoice. Registration and tax identifiers print as entered; tax amounts are not calculated. Logos are stored with the property; external image URLs and SVG are rejected.
