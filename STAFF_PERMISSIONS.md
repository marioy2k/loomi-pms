# Staff permissions

Settings → Staff & permissions lets Super Admins choose a role and grant or remove module actions.

- The protected owner is shown as Super Admin and cannot be edited.
- Super Admins have full action access and manage Admin and Employee accounts, including company features.
- Admins access all properties but only the module actions assigned to them. They can manage employees and may grant only permissions they themselves hold. They cannot edit their own account or any administrator account.
- Employees access assigned properties and selected actions.

Inventory and Point of Sale require their own permissions. Housekeeping does not grant Inventory, and Accounting does not grant POS. Permission changes take effect on the next server request. Administrators must reload an already-open page to refresh its visible controls.

Existing Admin records retain their previously saved action permissions. They now require a Super Admin to change administrator accounts or company features. The owner can promote an existing Admin to Super Admin explicitly.

This private site's sharing controls remain separate from staff module permissions. A staff record does not send an invitation or change site sharing.
