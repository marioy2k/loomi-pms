# Swimming day passes

Open Swimming day passes in the sidebar. A manager sets adult and child prices and pool information, then a cashier issues a voucher for a selected date and a group of up to 100 people. The property currency applies; each property has independent prices and vouchers.

Vouchers have a unique number, guest/group name, adult and child quantities, date, original prices and pool conditions. Print to paper or save as PDF, or download a standalone HTML voucher. Company details and uploaded logo appear on the voucher. Guests present the voucher number to staff; there is no public verification endpoint.

## Payment and entry

Collect cash, card or bank-transfer payment, or issue an unpaid voucher and collect its full payment later. Card/bank payments are recorded after external confirmation. Unpaid, upcoming, expired, cancelled and previously redeemed vouchers cannot be admitted. Gate staff look up the number, verify the group and redeem the entire group once. Dates use America/Paramaribo.

Enable Sell swimming day passes for cashiers and Check and redeem swimming vouchers for gate staff. POS cashiers and accounting staff can also sell passes. Pool pricing can be configured by Property, POS management or Payments staff. Cancellation/refund needs POS management or Payments permission. All permissions are limited to assigned properties; self-hosted licence checks apply.

## Accounting

Swimming day passes have their own income category. The valid date is the earned date; payments for future dates appear as advances. Payment collection does not recognize income a second time. A paid, unused voucher can be cancelled with a full refund using its original method. Unpaid cancellation reverses the receivable without creating cash. Redeemed passes cannot be cancelled. Perform the actual refund externally before confirming it in Loomi.

Pool receipts appear in Accounting, separately from the bar/restaurant POS drawer closing. No capacity limits, hour enforcement, QR scanner, re-entry or partial group redemption are included. Add opening hours and age rules to pool information for staff and guests. A voucher must have a positive total; complimentary passes are not included.
